# Corporate Claw reference reconstruction

The attached 1000009043.png is the visual authority. This is an interactive office simulation, with real geometry, movable agents, floor picking, conversations, training and dashboard preserved.

Desktop: 62px navy header (#172d3e), 274px warm ivory directory (#f3f0e9), 88px navy footer. Georgia serif 40px brand, 28px heading, 21px department rows; Arial controls. Five initial rows: CEO, Production, Sales, Marketing, Finance. Department rows open the real roster; search spans all active agents. Back returns to departments. Furnishings and settings remain available as compact footer actions. Mobile keeps the existing collapsible directory and touch navigation; controls have 44px targets. Visible keyboard focus, live loading/error text and reduced motion remain.

Office: elevated oblique camera, warm herringbone oak, ivory cutaway partitions with black framed glass, two rug-defined lounges (green/navy upholstery), central paired workstation islands, upper-right meeting room, café and bookcase at right, black marble reception and planted feature wall toward the front. Plants are individual 3D leaves. Text is drawn onto dedicated sign surfaces, never the reference image onto the room. Rounded upholstery, metal chair bases, monitor backs/screens, desk drawers, books, cups and warm practical lamps supply detail.

People: adult human proportions, dark tailored jackets/trousers, white shirts, lapels, ties, distinct skin/hair colors, articulated shoulders/elbows/hips/knees. No cartoon eye atlas. Geometry and animation must work on WebGPU and WebGL2. The supplied people are too small to recover individual faces or hidden backs; those are inferred and must not be described as exact likeness.

Validation: build and asset checks, rig/deformation tests, task-slot reachability, deployment Git SHA, desktop/mobile interface checks, actual rendered comparison when graphics is available. No pixel-perfect or photoreal acceptance without rendered evidence. No new UI library is needed; existing React, Lucide and Anime.js are sufficient.
